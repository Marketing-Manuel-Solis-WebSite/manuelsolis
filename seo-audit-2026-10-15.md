All counts are > 0 ✅. All are within expected ranges (30–120 per shard). No drastic drops.

**Notes:**
- The `publishable()` filter removes any URL whose path is in the 301 redirect table —
  prevents `<loc>` tags pointing to redirecting paths (e.g. defunct attorney slugs).
- The sitemap index derives `<lastmod>` from the real max date of each shard's entries, not
  the build timestamp — avoids false freshness signals.
- Landing count grew from 25 (original context) to 42 — expected growth as new
  city × service pages were added.
