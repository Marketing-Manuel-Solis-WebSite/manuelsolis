**⚠️ Minor — Attorney page JSON-LD strategy:** Both `<Script>` tags on the attorney page use `strategy="afterInteractive"`, meaning the JSON-LD is injected client-side after hydration. Recommend changing attorney page scripts to `strategy="beforeInteractive"` or removing `strategy` to match the landing page pattern. Low risk — Googlebot renders JS — but worth fixing for parity.
